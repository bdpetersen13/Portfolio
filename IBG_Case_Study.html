Behind the Scenes

Product leadership challenges, stakeholder alignment, and strategic pivots

Key Challenges

Technical Feasibility Risk

Navigated build vs. buy decision for 12+ system integrations. Built business case for custom solution vs. $800K/year vendor platform.

Adoption Resistance

Overcame "Excel is fine" mindset through phased rollout, power-user program, and demonstrating quick wins in first 30 days.

AI Model Trust

Built stakeholder confidence in ML forecasts through transparent model validation, A/B testing against manual forecasts, and explainable AI features.

Executive Buy-In

Secured $1.2M investment through ROI modeling, competitive analysis, and executive roadmap alignment across CFO, CIO, and SVP Merchandising.

Cross-Functional Leadership

Led a 28-person cross-functional team (engineers, data scientists, UX, and business) through agile product development. Established weekly sprint reviews, monthly exec steering committee, and embedded product manager with finance team.

Strategic Pivot

Product positioning shift from "replacement tool" to "intelligence augmentation" drove adoption breakthrough. Reframed go-to-market messaging from efficiency gains to strategic enablement, turning skeptical users into champions and accelerating org-wide rollout by 3 months.

Key Learnings

Strategic product lessons from leading enterprise AI/ML platform development

💡

Product Positioning Drives Adoption

How you position AI products determines adoption success. Framing our platform as "intelligence augmentation" vs. "workforce replacement" transformed resistant users into champions, achieving 100% adoption in 6 months. Product-market messaging is as critical as product-market fit.

🤖

Build vs. Buy Requires ROI Rigor

Led build vs. buy analysis comparing $1.2M custom platform to $800K/year vendor solution. Custom build won through 3-year TCO modeling, competitive differentiation analysis, and strategic platform extensibility. PMs must defend architectural decisions with business cases, not just feature comparisons.

⚡

Data-Driven Roadmap Prioritization

Used RICE framework to prioritize 40+ feature requests, shipping highest-impact features first. Real-time usage analytics (Mixpanel) drove post-launch iteration, with top 3 features accounting for 85% of user value. Ruthless prioritization and measurement beats feature bloat.

🎯

Executive Sponsorship is Non-Negotiable

Secured CFO, CIO, and SVP Merchandising as executive sponsors before development started. Monthly steering committee with C-suite maintained strategic alignment, unblocked integration bottlenecks, and drove org-wide adoption. Enterprise products require executive air cover—PMs must sell up and across, not just down.
