The Problem

Curbside today is a purely transactional experience—not the premium, relational one our members expect

Current-state curbside flow placeholder
No member context
Associates only see member names—no context for personalized, relational interactions. Generic communication risks being misinterpreted as rude or unhelpful.
Hidden order issues
Out-of-stock and poor-quality items aren't surfaced to associates or members during pickup. Members discover issues at home, leading to frustration and perception they were charged for items they didn't receive.
Zero recovery options
No substitutions, no delivery from nearby clubs, no shipping from fulfillment centers—leaving members empty-handed with no alternatives.

Members pay for premium service across all channels—club, online, curbside, delivery. But today's curbside experience is purely transactional. Associates lack member-aware context, order issues are hidden until members get home, and there are no recovery paths when items are unavailable. This breaks the promise of a premium membership experience.

Goals & Success Criteria

Transforming curbside from transactional to relational—empowering associates to create truly 1:1 engagement with members

Primary Goals

  • • Lift satisfaction: Improve NPS through personalized touchpoints
  • • Reduce perceived wait: Make the curbside experience feel faster through proactive communication
  • • Lower contact rate: Reduce complaints through better recovery and communication
  • • Improve recovery acceptance: Increase member approval of substitutions and alternatives

Guardrails

  • • Privacy & legal compliance: All personalization must respect member privacy and regulatory requirements
  • • Communication pacing: Rate limits and suppression windows to prevent message fatigue
  • • Associate cognitive load: Keep HUD lightweight and actionable, not overwhelming
  • • Actual wait time: Deeper associate engagement must not increase wait times for other members

Discovery Insights

Member Pain Points

  • • Perceived wait amplification: After 5 minutes, members are 2x more likely to perceive wait as 10+ minutes—even if they only waited 5:30
  • • No substitution options: Unavailable items mean members leave empty-handed—no substitutions, no delivery from nearby clubs, no FC shipping
  • • Lack of proactive communication: Members discover order issues at home, not during pickup, creating perception they paid for items they didn't receive

Associate Pain Points

  • • No member context: Can't personalize service without knowing tenure, preferences, history
  • • Manual workarounds: Time-consuming to look up member details in separate systems
  • • Hard to recover nil picks: No guidance on best substitutions or recovery tactics

Signals Available

Membership tenure Previous orders Category affinity Prior issues Plus status Service usage

Top Insight

Personalized, timely messages and associate cues can create "Hearts & Minds" moments in curbside—transforming a transactional pickup into a loyalty-building experience.

Solution Overview

Creating truly 1:1 relational experiences—recognizing, celebrating, and empowering members through personalized Next Best Actions

Recognize & Celebrate

Surface meaningful moments—10-year anniversary, 100th curbside order, birthdays. Make members feel seen and valued at exactly the right time with the right context.

Next Best Experience

Deliver Next Best Action, Next Best Category, Next Best Experience—e.g., bought pet items? Surface pet pharmacy benefits. Right time, right moment, right relevancy.

1:1 Associate Empowerment

Equip associates with member context to create genuine, personalized interactions—transforming every pickup into a relational brand moment, not a transaction.

Strategic Approach

Transform curbside from a transactional fulfillment moment into a strategic loyalty driver. Every pickup becomes an opportunity to demonstrate we know our members, celebrate their milestones, and empower them to maximize their membership value. By embedding personalization at this critical brand touchpoint, we create competitive advantage through relational "Hearts & Minds" moments at scale.

Key Learnings

💡

Right Message, Right Time > Volume

One well-timed, personalized message outperforms multiple generic ones. Quality of personalization matters more than quantity of touchpoints. Members respond to relevance, not frequency.

🔧

Associate Experience is the Fulcrum

The quality of the associate HUD directly determines member experience quality. If associates don't trust or use the tool, personalization fails. Investing in associate experience and training is critical to scaling member delight.

🎯

Recovery Personalization Beats Generic Promos

Context-aware recovery (intelligent substitutions based on member preferences) drives higher satisfaction and acceptance than blanket discounts or generic apologies. Members value thoughtfulness over compensation.

⚡

Start Simple, Evolve to Sophisticated

Simple business rules delivered immediate value and built stakeholder confidence. This creates the foundation and runway to invest in ML-powered response modeling. Perfect is the enemy of shipped.
